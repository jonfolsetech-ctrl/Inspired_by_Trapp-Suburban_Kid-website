02 / THE STORY

ORDINARY
STREETS.
UNORDINARY
VISION.

Some people see the suburbs and think small. I see the first scene. Quiet streets, porch lights, parking lots after midnight—and a head full of plans that don't fit the neighborhood.

INSPIRED BY TRAPP is a point of view: the tension between where you come from and where you refuse to stop. The music lives where ambition meets anxiety, where the night feels wide open, and where every setback becomes another reason to build.

No borrowed story. No overnight-success fairytale. Just the work, the sound, and the decision to keep going.
